# Binder 4.1: guides you can see, and no Binder VPN

## Why

Binder VPN is cancelled: the app, its sign-in service and the Friends panel go. The guides added in 4.0 were a line
of text at the top of a screen; 4.1 makes them full-window walkthroughs with pictures of the screen they explain, so a
new owner sees what they are setting up before they do it.

## Binder VPN goes

- `vpn/` (the menu bar app and the service), `friends.rs`, `vpn_client.rs`, the `friends_*` commands, the Friends
  panel, `vpn_dir` and `vpn_address`, the `vpn` Makefile targets, the README and site sections and their images.
- The VPN screen stays: the owner's own exit nodes as regions.
- A config file that still has `vpn_dir` or `vpn_address` loads; they are dropped on the next save.

## Pictures

- `app/src/assets/guide/<screen>.jpg`: each screen as it looks with the demo data, 2400 by 1500, made by
  `scripts/guide-shots.sh` from `make preview` with headless Chrome. They ship in the app; nothing is fetched.
- The preview takes flags after the screen, for the pictures and for checking the guides by eye:
  `#screen=<name>&setup` (nothing set up: no server, no Tailscale, no gh, no Cloud, no printer), `&welcome` (the
  first-run setup), `&guide` (the screen's guide).

## First run

The setup fills the window. On the left: the mascot, the steps as a list (done ones checked), the step's title, what
it is for and its one control. On the right: a large picture, in a window frame, of the screen the step unlocks
(Overview, Servers, Projects, GitHub, then Overview again). Footer: Skip setup, Back, Skip this step, the main
button. Escape skips. Steps and checks are the 4.0 ones.

## Screen guides

- The first time a screen opens, its guide covers the window: the screen's picture, its name, one line on what it is
  for, three short points on what it shows and does, and what it needs with Ready or Not yet from `setup_checks`
  and the config.
- Got it closes it (`tips_hidden`, as before). When something is missing, Set it up closes it too and leaves the
  screen's setup steps in view.
- `?` (outside a text field) opens the guide of the screen in view. Settings › Setup › Show guides again.

## Setup steps

`NeedsSetup` becomes a centered card: an icon, the title, one line on what the screen does, numbered steps where
every command is a code block with Copy, the action, and below it a dimmed picture of the screen once it works.
Used where a screen can't work yet: Servers, Sites and Agents without a server, Sites with no sites, Cloud without the
share, Printer without a printer, VPN without Tailscale, GitHub without `gh`.

## Not now

Animated walkthroughs, pointing at parts of a screen, translations.
