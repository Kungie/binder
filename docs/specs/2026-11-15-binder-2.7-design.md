# Binder 2.7: the morning brief, GitHub's issues and checks, a hand on the containers, Sites, the week in a note, and a fuller New task

Status: waiting for the owner's review. Builds on the 2.0 to 2.5 specs and the 2.6 changes (which had no spec of their own: the always-on printer panel, the animations, `done_log`, pointer drags on the board, the fixed signing identity), which still hold wherever this one is silent. Where they disagree, this one wins.

## Why

- Binder now works while the owner sleeps (night tasks, the container watch), but what happened overnight is scattered or lost: a notification that was missed is gone.
- GitHub is where issues and CI live, and the owner checks it by hand for each repo.
- When a container alert arrives, Binder can't show why or do anything about it; the owner opens a terminal.
- A print can finish or fail unnoticed.
- Six websites run on the home server, and nothing shows at a glance whether they are up and what they cost.
- The week's work is spread over daily files; there is no summary to look back on.
- New task sets only the project, assignee and priority; the rest means opening the task again. Its Create task button sits crammed at the end of a full row and looks about to spill out.

## Goals

1. **Morning brief.** The first time Binder opens after 05:00, Overview starts with a card of what happened since the owner last looked.
2. **GitHub.** Open issues assigned to the owner become tasks in their project; a project shows its open pull requests and its last CI result.
3. **Containers.** On the Server screen a container can show its recent log and be restarted, after a confirmation.
4. **Print notifications.** A Mac notification when a print finishes or fails.
5. **Sites.** A screen of the websites on the server: a live preview, whether each answers, and small CPU, memory and disk figures. Watching only.
6. **Weekly note.** When a week ends, a summary of it goes to the vault.
7. **3D Printer.** The sidebar item and its screen are called `3D Printer`, with a 3D printer icon.
8. **New task.** Everything a task has can be set while creating it, and the dialog's buttons have room of their own.

## Non-goals

- Deploying sites, editing them, or starting and stopping their containers.
- Stopping, removing or starting containers. Restart is the only action.
- Writing to GitHub: creating issues, commenting, reviewing or merging.
- Notifications on the phone, or a brief anywhere but Overview.

## Anti-goals

- A restart without a confirmation, or one the owner didn't ask for.
- Duplicate tasks from GitHub, or a sync that overwrites what the owner changed in a task.
- Reading or showing the tunnels' tokens or credentials while finding the sites.
- A notification flood: one notification per print outcome.
- A weekly note written over one that exists.
- The Sites screen loading pages while it isn't on screen.

## Approaches considered (decided with the owner)

- **Sites: watch or deploy.** (a) Watch only, chosen. (b) Deploy too: each site deploys differently (a copied `public/` folder, a `git pull` in `repo/`, a Docker build), so it would double the work.
- **Site previews.** (a) A live, scaled-down page in the card, chosen: always current and no load on the server. (b) Screenshots by headless Chromium on the Pi every hour: a heavy container on a small board, and pictures up to an hour old. (c) Screenshots taken on the Mac in the background: awkward and fragile in Tauri.

## 1. Morning brief

- Binder keeps a log of events: container alerts, print outcomes and server restarts. Night and day runs are already recorded. Events are logged whether or not their notification switch is on; the switches only decide what becomes a notification.
- The Mac often sleeps at night, so the watch can't see everything as it happens. After a gap (the first check after more than 5 minutes without one), Binder reads what the server remembers instead:
  - the server's uptime: a boot after the last check is a restart at that time;
  - each container's `StartedAt`, `FinishedAt`, `RestartCount` and state: a container that stopped, started again or restarted during the gap gets its events logged with the server's times.
- A restart the owner asks for from Binder is logged as `restarted by you` and sends no alert.
- The brief covers everything since the owner last dismissed a brief, or the last 24 hours if they never did:
  - agent runs that ended: `ORB-3 done` or `LAB-2 failed`, night runs marked with the moon;
  - container alerts, the latest per container: `web stopped at 02:14, running again at 02:20`;
  - server restarts: `The server restarted at 04:02`;
  - prints that finished or failed;
  - GitHub issues newly assigned and brought in as tasks.
- It shows at the top of Overview from the first time Binder is open after 05:00 local time until the owner presses **Dismiss**, or until 12:00. With nothing to say, there is no card.
- Each line links to what it is about: a task opens its sheet, a container opens the Server screen.

## 2. GitHub

- On every GitHub sync (at start and on **Sync**), Binder asks `gh` for the open issues assigned to the owner, in repos that are projects in Binder.
  - An issue that has no task gets one in its project: its title, its body as notes, Todo, assigned to the owner, with the issue's number and link kept on the task. The task shows `#12` and opens the issue from its sheet.
  - Binder never changes such a task afterwards, except: when the issue is closed on GitHub, or is no longer assigned to the owner, the task moves to Done (unless it is already Done or Canceled).
  - A task deleted by the owner stays deleted: its issue is remembered and not brought in again.
- Each GitHub project also gets its count of open pull requests and the result of the latest CI run on its default branch: `Passing`, `Failing` or `Running`, or nothing when the repo has no runs. The project card and the project page show them, the CI result as a small coloured icon (online green, error red, in progress yellow).
- Without `gh` or its login, nothing changes and nothing breaks, as today.

## 3. Containers

- On the Server screen, pressing a container opens a panel under the list with its last 200 log lines (`docker logs --tail 200`, both streams), newest at the bottom, in mono, with **Refresh** and **Restart**.
- **Restart** asks `Restart web? Its connections drop for a moment.` with **Restart** and **Cancel**. Restarting shows `Restarting…` and then the new status.
- The container's name reaches the shell only through `remote::quote`.

## 4. Print notifications

- When a printer goes from printing (or paused) to finished: `<job> is done`, with the time it took. To failed: `<job> failed`, with the layer it reached.
- One notification per outcome, sent by the running app while it is connected to the printer; it is recorded for the brief.
- A switch in Settings › 3D Printer, **Notify me about prints**, on by default.

## 5. Sites

- A **Sites** item in the sidebar, under Server, with its own screen.
- Binder finds the sites on the server: each folder in `~/sites` with a `docker-compose.yml` is one site, named after the folder.
  - Its address comes from a `hostname:` line in `cloudflared/config.yml` when there is one. Binder reads only those lines, never tokens, credentials or `.env` files. A site with no address found shows **Add address**, and the address the owner types is kept in `config.toml`. The owner can change it there too.
- Each site is a card in a grid:
  - the live preview: the site loaded at 1280 × 800 and shown scaled down, not clickable, loaded only while the card is on screen;
  - the name, the address (opens in the browser), and a dot: green when the address answers within 5 seconds with a status below 500 and its containers run, red otherwise, with the reason on hover;
  - one small mono line: `CPU 0.4% · RAM 38 MB · Disk 12 MB` (the site's containers added together, and its folder on disk).
- A site that refuses to be shown inside another page shows its name and icon instead of the preview.
- Figures are read every 30 seconds while the screen is open, in one SSH call.

## 6. Weekly note

- A week is an ISO week, Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 local time. The first time Binder runs after a week has ended, it writes `Weekly/<year>-W<week>.md` in the vault for that week, so the note is complete. Weeks missed while Binder didn't run are written too, up to four weeks back; events are kept for five weeks.
  - the done lines of the week, by project, from Binder's own log;
  - the tasks closed that week: those moved to Done or Canceled, by hand or by GitHub, counted by when they closed (a new `closed` time on tasks; tasks closed before 2.7 count by their last change). A task GitHub closes adds no done line;
  - the agent runs that ended, with how many failed;
  - the count of container alerts and prints.
- Without the vault, it is written when the vault is back, still for its own week. An existing file is never overwritten.

## 7. 3D Printer

- The sidebar item reads `3D Printer`, with an icon of a 3D printer (frame, gantry and nozzle) drawn like the other sidebar icons. The screen's heading reads the same. The Overview panel stays `Printer`.

## 8. New task

- The dialog sets everything the task sheet does: title, notes, status, priority, project, assignee, night, and the due date and time. Each starts as today: status from where it was opened (a board column's Todo, In progress and so on, otherwise Todo), the project from the screen, the assignee from the view, priority none, no notes, not a night task, no due date.
- Layout, in the dialog's 560 px:
  - the title, large, then the notes as a growing field (`Add notes`) under it;
  - one row of chips, the same controls as the sheet's: status, priority, assignee, project, due, and the moon for night. A chip that wraps goes to a second row; nothing is cut off or overflows;
  - a footer under a hairline: the error on the left, **Cancel** and **Create task** on the right, the same height, inside the dialog's padding.
- The same rules as the sheet: turning on night assigns the agent; giving the task to Me turns night off. A due time needs a date.
- Enter in the title creates; in the notes it starts a new line. ⌘Enter creates from anywhere. Escape cancels.

## Left to the builder

- How the event log is stored and pruned, as long as the brief and the weekly note can read a week of it.
- Which `gh` commands give the issues, pull requests and runs, and how they are batched.
- How the site status check reaches the address (from the app, not the server).

## Calls I made (owner, please check)

- The brief shows from 05:00 until dismissed or 12:00, and only when there is something in it.
- Issues assigned to you come in as tasks with your assignee and Todo; closing or unassigning the issue moves the task to Done.
- CI is the latest run on the default branch only.
- Restart is the only container action, always with a confirmation.
- Print notifications have their own switch, on by default.
- A site's dot needs both: the address answers and its containers run.
- The weekly note is written after the week ends (the first run from Monday 00:00), not on Sunday evening, so it holds the whole week; missed weeks are filled in up to four weeks back. It goes into `Weekly/` at the vault's root.
- After the Mac sleeps, the brief still shows the night: Binder reads the server's uptime and the containers' start, stop and restart times.
- Events are logged even when their notifications are off.
- A restart you start from Binder is logged as yours and sends no alert.
- A task's close time is kept from now on; older tasks count by their last change.
- The Overview panel keeps the shorter name `Printer`.
- New task uses the sheet's own chips rather than new controls, its buttons go in a footer, and ⌘Enter creates from the notes.
